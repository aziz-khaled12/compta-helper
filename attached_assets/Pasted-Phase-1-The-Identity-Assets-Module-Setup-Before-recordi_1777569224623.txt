Phase 1: The "Identity & Assets" Module (Setup)Before recording a single sale, your app needs to know the "Starting Line."Company Profile Setup: Create a form to input the Entity Name (e.g., EURL DJERDJERA), NIF, and AI.  Capital Initialization: Build a "Funding" input for Personal Contributions (fonds propres) and Bank Loans (Emprunts).  Fixed Asset Ledger: Create a module for "Equipments" where the user inputs the purchase price ($2,700,000$ DA) and the lifespan ($5$ years).  Automated Amortization: Write a function to calculate monthly depreciation ($45,000$ DA/month) which will automatically post to the "Autres Charges" table.  Phase 2: The "Journal" Module (Daily Inputs)This is where the user will spend most of their time.Purchases & Suppliers Form:Input: Date, Label, HT Amount, TVA (19%), and Payment Method (Cash/Bank/Credit).  Logic: Automatically calculate TVA Déductible ($57,000$ DA) and TTC.  Sales & Customers Form:Input: Quantity sold, Unit Price HT ($400$ DA), and TVA.  Logic: Automatically calculate TVA Collectée ($152,000$ DA) and total TTC ($952,000$ DA).  Operational Expenses Form: A simple input for Rent, Electricity, and various supplies.  Phase 3: The Inventory & Consumption EngineStock Ledger (In/Out):"In": Automatically pull from the Purchases form.  "Out": Create a "Consumption" trigger. When the user says they used $200,000$ DA of raw materials, the app must subtract this from the Stock Balance and add it to Expenses.  Finished Goods Tracking: Logic to record the production of units ($2,000$ units) and their cost of production ($560,000$ DA).  Phase 4: The Payroll Module (Bulletin de Paie)Employee Profiles: Store Name, Family Situation (Married + 2 children), and Grade/Echelon.  Salary Logic Engine:Calculate Salaire de Poste ($58,220$ DA) based on Base Salary + Experience + Bonuses.  Calculate CNAS ($9\%$) and IRG based on the official Algerian scale.  Output: A generated PDF "Bulletin de Paie" as shown in 02- Etude de cas _260430_171450.pdf.  Phase 5: The Reporting Engine (The Outputs)This is the "Magic" part that replaces the accountant.Table Aggregator: Write queries to pull data into the specialized tables:Caisse/Banque: Summarize all "Cash/Bank" tags from the Journal.  Client/Fournisseur: Summarize "Reste à payer" for credit transactions.  The Financial Statements:Income Statement (Compte de Résultat): Subtract total expenses (including $45,000$ DA amortization) from total sales to show the Net Result ($240,000$ DA).  Balance Sheet (Bilan): Match Assets (Cash + Bank + Stock + Net Equipment) against Liabilities (Capital + Result + Loans + Debt).  Phase 6: Tax Compliance (G50 & G12)G50 Generator: Create a logic to calculate the monthly tax debt:$TVA = TVA Collectée - TVA Déductible$.  Include the IRG/Salarié total from the Payroll module.  G12 Generator: A year-to-date summary of the Chiffre d'Affaires (Turnover) for the IFU tax filing.
